# Experimental templates

FP02 installs **zero application templates**. The proposed family IDs in the frozen local mapping
are selectors, not availability claims. FP03 adds actual versioned reusable source templates
here and registers their exact identities through the [assembly API](../README.md).

Keep shared project files under one explicit `local.project` assembly owner. Never emit fake
successful business handlers or transcode results. Preserve named custom extensions and every
unimplemented/unsupported setting in the partial report. No broad certification suite is required
before the first field build/smoke; strict readiness remains a separate unchanged boundary.
