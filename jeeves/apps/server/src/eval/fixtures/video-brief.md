Please design a video sharing site for me on a blank board.

Creators sign in and upload their videos. Videos can be large, so an upload must survive a dropped connection and carry on where it stopped. Each upload is processed in the background into streamable versions with a thumbnail, and a video becomes watchable only once processing has finished.

Anyone can watch without an account, but viewers may sign in. Video details should be served from a cache. Viewers can search the published videos, every video shows how many times it has been watched, and signed-in viewers can keep a list of favorite videos.

Name things as you see fit. When it's done, turn on Runnable processes.
