Please design a podcast app for me on a blank board.

Hosts sign in and upload the audio for each episode. The files are big, so an upload has to resume after a dropped connection. Every episode is processed in the background into a streaming format with its cover art, and listeners only see an episode once its processing has finished.

Anyone can browse and play published episodes without an account; listeners may sign in. Episode pages should load from a cache. Listeners can search the published episodes, each episode shows its play count, and signed-in listeners can follow the shows they like and see the list of shows they follow.

Name things as you see fit. When it's done, turn on Runnable processes.
